About Us

CodeStash is a free, curated vault of developer resources: public APIs, hosting platforms, UI component libraries, icon sets and everyday tools. Everything listed here is free to use or has a genuinely useful free tier.

Why we built it

Developers lose hours hunting through scattered bookmarks, outdated “awesome lists” and search results full of ads. CodeStash puts the good stuff in one fast, clean place, so you can find the right tool and get back to building.

Why it’s helpful

  • Hand-picked: every resource is chosen for quality, documentation and real-world usefulness.
  • Instant search: filter by name, category or keyword as you type.
  • My Stash: bookmark favourites and keep them in your own browser, with no account or sign-up needed.
  • Fast and lightweight: a single page that works great on phones and desktops.

What you’ll find

  • APIs: free and public APIs for weather, data, images, crypto and more.
  • Hosting: platforms to deploy frontends, backends and databases for free.
  • UI Components: frameworks and libraries to ship polished interfaces faster.
  • Icons: open-source icon packs for every project.
  • Tools: editors, testers, converters and references for daily dev work.

Built by a developer

CodeStash is built and maintained by an independent developer and is open-source on GitHub. Know a great free resource that’s missing? Tell us and we’ll take a look.
